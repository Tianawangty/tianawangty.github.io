## Corrections Made to My Own Copy

Three errors in the JMP description, found by checking the draft against `SteeringShift.md` and `quality_reports/handoff/2026-08-21_advisor-feasibility-causal-chain.md` in the paper repo:

| Error | What the source says | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Wrote identification as county-level or state-level throughout | Handoff §2: downstream grain is **category × month, national**. Losing the county dimension downstream is the whole point of the restructure — the geographic mismatch is resolved by identifying across categories, not counties | Third paragraph rewritten to state each stage's level and why it is set by where the outcome is observed |
| "Nielsen retail scanner data spanning 2006 to 2024" | Handoff §3: "the estimation window is separately capped at 2022 by the dose, which runs 2006 to 2022"; 18 announcement events in that span | Window moved out of the data sentence and stated as "eighteen annual adjustments between 2006 and 2022" |
| "county-by-month agricultural employment and payroll" | Handoff §4: employment is monthly, **payroll is quarterly** | "county-level agricultural employment by month and payroll by quarter" |

One further softening, made before the pass: "imposed from outside the market… an unusually clean opportunity" became "imposed from outside the individual farm… a well-defined setting". The AEWR is mechanically endogenous, set from the prior year's regional FLS average, and the design explicitly does not require its exogeneity. The original wording invited exactly the objection the design was built to avoid.
