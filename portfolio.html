Text as data

Topic modeling and sentiment analysis of public discussion.

Topic and sentiment shift around the 2019 Hong Kong bill withdrawal

This project studies discussion around Hong Kong’s 2019 extradition bill withdrawal using four million tweets collected over four months. The structural topic model (STM) captures how positive and negative tweets use different words to discuss the same topic. The analysis examines how these topic and sentiment patterns change before and after the withdrawal.

Machine learning at the IMF

Projects from the IMF’s innovation challenges.

A Machine Learning Toolbox for Climate Policy Analysis

International Monetary Fund · Winning project, IMF Climate Innovation Challenge

The fourth component of this toolbox uses labeled records from the Climate Policy Database to train a topic model for identifying policy themes in new text. Existing policy categories guide what the model learns and provide a reference for checking its output. The work covers the fifty largest emitters and includes an index of climate policy intensity.

Launched at the IMF Annual Meetings, October 2023.

Does Sentiment Lead Market Stress?

International Monetary Fund · Winning project, IMF Big Data for Surveillance Challenge

This early warning project examines whether sentiment helps predict financial market stress. The work includes a currency-pressure index covering forty-eight countries and thirty years of monthly data. The index is checked against three published historical records of currency crises.

Project presented at the Challenge Finale, IMF Annual Meetings, October 2019.
