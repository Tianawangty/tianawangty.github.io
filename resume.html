Even when the question that pulls me in is a broad one, I enjoy bringing it down to something concrete enough to measure. The questions are familiar ones, like how fast prices are rising, whether it is supply or demand, or whether policy is making it worse. For example, a single inflation number is an average over thousands of local markets that moved for different reasons, and averaging them together throws away exactly the variation that tells you why. So I work from the bottom up, at whatever resolution the question actually lives at. That might be a ZIP code or a county, a week or a month, a single UPC or a product category. I work with designs that separate cause from coincidence. If you are starting something in AI or data science and want someone to build it with, write to me.
